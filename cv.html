Scientific Activities

My research focuses on high-content biomedical image analysis, integrative computational pathology, omics imaging, spatial transcriptomics, multimodal biomedical AI and responsible artificial intelligence. The central goal is to design AI methods that connect tissue morphology, molecular information and clinical interpretation in a trustworthy and clinically meaningful way.

Main Recent Projects

  • SIMAI: Synergizing Mechanistic and AI Approaches for Modeling the Impact of Microenvironment Heterogeneity on Immunotherapy Efficacy, MIC 2025 grant, 2025–2029.
  • STRATIFIAD: Refining Alzheimer Disease Patients’ stratification using effective, traceable and explicable AI in computational histopathology, Big Brain Theory grant, Paris Brain Institute, 2021–2023.
  • MALMO: Mathematical Approaches to Modelling Metabolic Plasticity and Heterogeneity in Melanoma, AVIESAN / ITMO Cancer grant, 2020–2023.
  • PAPHOS: Platform for Advanced Prescriptive Health Operational System, EIT Health grant, 2016–2018.
  • FlexMIm: Collaborative digital histopathology, FUI grant, French Ministry of Industry, 2013–2016.
